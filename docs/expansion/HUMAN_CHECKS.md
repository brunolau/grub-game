# HUMAN_CHECKS.md - what only people can check in Club & Grub 2.0.0

Status: the check list of plan step P4.3-P4.5 (docs/expansion/PLAN.md 7) and of DESIGN.md G90. Author: lead designer, 2026-10-09 (phase 4); brought up to the release tree by the release integrator the same day (lines X-02, X-03, V-08, V-10, V-15, V-16, W-02, C-64 and section D: what the last round of the phase changed or measured). **Nothing in this file has been done by a person yet.** 2.0.0 is built and proven by tools - the gate proofs, the route replays, the bot sets, the audits (PLAN.md 6-8) - and no tool can play as two people, hear a loop or hold eight keys on a cheap keyboard. Those checks are here, each as one line a person can tick, with what a tool DID measure about it and the file and value to change if it fails. No agent has faked any of them; a box is ticked by the person who did the check.

## 0. Before you start

- **The build**: the 2.0.0 Windows build as `tools/build_windows.ps1` and `tools/build_installer.ps1` leave it (docs/BUILD.md 3) - `build\windows\ClubAndGrub.exe` (one file), `build\ClubAndGrub-2.0.0-windows.zip`, the installer `build\ClubAndGrub-2.0.0-setup.exe` - or the preview folder (`Desktop\pre2_preview\Play.cmd`) once the release build has refreshed it. A pair that just plays needs nothing else: Play > Co-op. No debug build is kept and none would help (the flow runner and `--perf` are development files left out of every export): the recording of a cheat route and the performance runs of section D need the project and its editor binary - `GD_VISIBLE=1 GD_TIMEOUT=7200 bash .tools/gd.sh play ...` (a window with sound; without `GD_VISIBLE=1` the window is off-screen and muted).
- **The people**: for sections P and C a mixed-skill pair - one who plays platformers, one who rarely does - who have NOT seen the co-op stages; for section X one player who knows the rules of DESIGN.md D and is told to cheat; for section V two to four players. An observer who does not help takes the notes.
- **Reaching a stage**: Title > Play > Co-op > the book > the join panel. Book II is open from the start. There is no stage select for two players and co-op stages have no codes: play a campaign in order, keep the save - its world map replays every stage it has finished. (Measured: the debug flow commands `start_level <id> players=2` and `input device` start a stage without menus, but only P1 gets a device - with D and Num 6 held P1 walked and P2 stood still; the devices of a party are given out by the join panel. So a pair goes through the menus.) Saves and settings live in `%APPDATA%\ClubAndGrub\`.
- **Difficulty**: Beginner plays worlds 1-3 and 5-7 (22 co-op stages), Expert all 35. Run the mixed pair on Beginner first, then a practised pair on Expert.
- **Notes**: tick the box when the check passes. When it fails, write the check's number, the difficulty and what happened; do not fix on the spot. A value marked *(tune)* is changed in docs/expansion/DESIGN.md D.12 first, then in its constant; D.12's last column says what must be run again after the change (gates = `bash tools/world_coop_gates.sh`, about 50 minutes, alone, and `tools/sp_identity.sh`; routes = `bash .tools/gd.sh test coop_routes`; bots = `bash tools/g3_versus_bots.sh`).
- **Units**: 1 s = 24.28 ticks; 12 ticks = half a second, 24 = one second, 73 = 3 s, 243 = 10 s. A cell is 16 px.
- **What "Measured" means**: a number a tool produced on the release tree, with the way to repeat it. It is never a substitute for the check beside it.

## P. The rules of the tribe (a pair, once, in co-op 1-1 or 5-1)

Try each on the first stage before the stage list. Measured for the whole section: `bash .tools/gd.sh test player`, `test world` and `test enemies_coop` pin every rule below tick by tick; the checks here are whether people read them.

- [ ] **P-01** **The join panel.** Both press Jump on their own device, pick a colour with Left / Right and hold Strike one second. **Look for:** it works without reading a manual; each player knows which hero is his (the P1 / P2 tags, the colours). **If it fails:** `scripts/ui/join.gd`; the texts `UI_JOIN_*` in `locale/en.po`; `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `JOIN_READY_HOLD_TICKS` (24).
- [ ] **P-02** **The egg.** One hero falls into a pit: he floats in an egg that drifts after his partner; its owner nudges it Left / Right; the partner whacks it (any hit, a throw, a head bounce) and he hatches with 2 hearts (Beginner) / 1 (Expert), blinking. **Look for:** the downed player knows he is not out; the partner knows what to do; the hatch never drops the hatched hero into the same pit. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `HATCH_HEARTS_BEGINNER` (2) / `_EXPERT` (1), `HATCH_BLINK_TICKS` (44), `EGG_NUDGE_PX` (1); the sign `SIGN_COOP_W1_EGG`.
- [ ] **P-03** **The Beginner egg.** On Beginner let the egg follow for a whole stretch. **Look for:** it keeps up (2 px a tick, 6 beyond 64 px) without covering what the partner must see; the egg's player is not bored - the stars it shows over hidden spots give him something to do. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `EGG_DRIFT_PX` (2), `EGG_DRIFT_FAST_PX` (6), `EGG_OFFSET_X` / `_Y` (-24, -48), `EGG_SCOUT_RADIUS_PX` (32).
- [ ] **P-04** **The Expert egg.** On Expert leave an egg unhatched for 10 s. **Look for:** it flies to the checkpoint and waits; both players understand why; going back for it is a fair price. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `EGG_RETURN_TICKS_EXPERT` (243).
- [ ] **P-05** **The leash.** One hero walks on while the other stays: an edge arrow with a stone countdown, then the one outside the view becomes an egg after 5 s (Beginner) / 3 s (Expert); no life is lost. **Look for:** the countdown is seen in time; the slower player does not feel punished for the faster one's hurry. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `LEASH_EGG_TICKS_BEGINNER` (121) / `_EXPERT` (73).
- [ ] **P-06** **The idle partner.** One player puts his pad down. **Look for:** after 7 s a small "z" bubble warns, after 10 s he dozes ("Zzz"); the other hero now passes through his head, a plate under him is not held; any key wakes him at once. A player who holds a plate by CROUCHING never dozes, however long he waits. **Measured:** `bash .tools/gd.sh test player_idle`. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `IDLE_TICKS` (243), `IDLE_WARN_TICKS` (170) - gates and routes after a change.
- [ ] **P-07** **No dead end beside an idle partner.** Hero A goes down (an egg); player B puts his pad down; A keeps nudging his egg. **Look for:** B dozes after 10 s; 3 s later the curtain falls, one life is paid and both stand at the checkpoint - is 3 s right, and is it clear what happened (nothing on the screen counts the 3 s down)? Then repeat with BOTH pads on the table: nothing may happen, however long. Then with B waking in the 3 s: no wipe. **Measured:** the clock is the party's rule test (`bash .tools/gd.sh test world_party`). **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `IDLE_WIPE_TICKS` (73; 0 switches the rule off).
- [ ] **P-08** **The ward mark is seen.** At the first ward (1-1: the sign before the lake shore; 5-1: the sign before the mesa cliff) look at the beasts. **Look for:** the chalk stripes are noticed without being pointed at, from a normal seat, also in a small window (320 x 180 art pixels shown 2x); they do not look like a hurt flash, a Shaman's bone or a daze; they hold on pale beasts (snow turtles, ghosts, gulls) and on dark ones; a beast that walks out of the ward loses them. Its builder looked at off-screen screenshots and says where to look hardest: on pale beasts (the white gull, the white ghost, the snow turtle, the grey Bull Rex) the chalk vanishes into the body and the two ink lines alone carry it; on the smallest bodies (the bat, the Raptor) one band crosses a 30 px body; and it is a pattern, not a symbol - do two people who were told nothing connect the stripes with "no bounce" after the sign? Try the pale beasts and the bat at arm's length on a TV. **Measured:** the mark is one shared material on the enemy's own sprite (`scripts/fx/ward_mark.gd`: chalk 246 / 240 / 222 between ink lines, a band every 26 art px) and adds no draw call (19 with it, 19 without, on a view of 6 marked and 6 unmarked beasts); painted on the first standing frame of each of the 55 enemy sheets (`bash .tools/gd.sh script res://build/party/wf12/mark_census.gd`) it covers 21-32 % of the body, 50 sheets are strong (chalk step 0.30 or more, two or more bands), on 3 the ink lines alone carry it (bear_b, sea_snail, octopus) and 2 hold one thin band (fish, fish_b); it changes no tick (all 57 co-op route runs replay to the same digests, `tests/test_world_ward_mark.gd`); 186 of the 396 enemy records of the co-op files stand in a ward (`python build/lead_design/wf12/wards.py`). **If it fails:** `scripts/fx/ward_mark.gd` `BAND_PERIOD` (26), `CHALK_PX` (5), `EDGE_PX` (2), `CHALK_COLOUR`, `INK_COLOUR` - drawing only, nothing to re-prove.
- [ ] **P-09** **A marked beast is no stepping stone.** Jump on a marked beast holding Jump. **Look for:** a dust puff and a thud, no bounce: the hero falls through it; it does not hurt him on the way down nor for half a second after he lands; if he stays inside it longer, it hurts. Does this read as a rule ("marked: no step") or as a bug? Does the pair then use each other's shoulders, as the sign says? **Measured:** `bash .tools/gd.sh test player_gate_rules` (the fall with a body under him is the free fall tick for tick; the grace is 12 ticks after the landing). **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `WARD_GRACE_TICKS` (12) - gates and routes; the cue is `fx/dust` and `Sfx.LAND` in `scripts/player/player.gd` `_ward_stomp`.
- [ ] **P-10** **The edge of a ward.** Lead a beast across the first column of a ward (1-1: column 66, left of the sign) and bounce on it right there. **Look for:** the mark goes on and off at the edge; within about one body width of the edge the mark can be wrong (the mark follows the beast's column, the rule the hero's) - is that ever noticed, and does it ever matter? **If it fails:** nothing in 2.0.0 (accepted, DESIGN.md G85 #4: every edge lies 12+ cells from its gate); a wider `ward=` on that tablet moves the edge away from where people bounce.
- [ ] **P-11** **Unmarked beasts still bounce.** Outside the wards bounce on a beast with and without Jump held. **Look for:** the 1.0 bounce is whole (the small hop; 6 tiles with Jump held); after two warded stages a pair still tries it. **If it fails:** the pair has learned "never bounce": the mark is too common - `levels/<id>_coop.lvl` `ward=` (gates).
- [ ] **P-12** **Shoulder Hop and Totem Ride.** Land on the partner's head with Jump held (the hop: thrown up) and without (the ride: stand on him; he walks, the rider strikes; Down + Jump drops off). **Look for:** the two are told apart after a few tries; the carrier's small jumps do not shed the rider by surprise. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `TOTEM_DROP_LOCK_TICKS` (12); the signs `SIGN_COOP_W1_HOP` / `SIGN_W5_COOP_HOP`.
- [ ] **P-13** **The count-in.** At any twin gate (drums, a bond) stand ready. **Look for:** three blips and "go" are heard over the music and the effects at the default volumes; nobody needs to count aloud. **If it fails:** the `db` of `Sfx.COUNT_IN` in `scripts/core/audio_table.gd`; `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `COUNT_IN_SPACING_TICKS` (8).
- [ ] **P-14** **Batter Up.** One curls (Down + Swap, up to 66 ticks), the other strikes him: forward = a line drive, high = a lob, low = a grounder; a crouch-charged strike flies 1.5 times as far. **Look for:** the curl is found from the sign; the batter aims on purpose after three balls; being the ball is fun, not a punishment. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/party_tuning.gd` `CURL_MAX_TICKS` (66), `BAT_LINE_DRIVE_XVEL` / `_YVEL` (144, -128), `BAT_LOB_XVEL` / `_YVEL` (32, -240) - gates and routes.
- [ ] **P-15** **Helper mode** (Options > Co-op). **Look for:** P2 is not hurt by enemies but still falls into pits; every gate still needs him. A small child can play P2 through 1-1. **If it fails:** `scripts/ui/options_panel.gd`, PHYSICS.md C.12 "Helper mode".
- [ ] **P-16** **Joining and leaving in the middle of a stage** (the pause menu). **Look for:** the stage restarts at the checkpoint in the other layout with the score kept, and nobody is surprised by it. **If it fails:** `scripts/core/scene_flow.gd`; the pause menu's texts in `locale/en.po`.
- [ ] **P-17** **The HUD for two.** **Look for:** P2 finds his hearts and his belt (top right) at once; the tribe lives are understood as shared; a boss bar leaves both panels readable. **If it fails:** `scripts/ui/hud.gd`.

## How this list was made, and what it does not cover

- Written by the lead designer from DESIGN.md D / E, the 35 co-op level files and the rulings G71-G92; the numbers come from tools run on the release tree: `build/lead_design/wf12/pair_probe.gd` (the 57 recorded two-stream route runs: per stage the length and the hurts, per gate the ticks from the tablet to the far cell), `build/lead_design/wf12/wards.py` (the wards and the enemy records in them), `build/lead_design/wf12/music.py` (the track lengths), `build/lead_design/wf12/signs.py` (the signs inside wards), `build/lead_design/wf12/feast_probe.gd` (line X-03), the audit's `tools/audit_assets.py`, the gate job and the bot sets of PLAN.md 6. `build/` is not versioned: the three small tools are scratch files of the phase and are named here so that their numbers can be made again.
- The stage lines are generated from the level files' own gate names: `docs/spec/test_spec_docs.py` fails when a gate of a co-op file has no line here, when a line names a gate that no file has, or when an arena or a music file of the game is missing from sections V and M.
- Not covered: iOS and macOS devices beyond the export presets (no toolchain in the project, DESIGN.md G88), localisation other than English, and anything a later version adds.
