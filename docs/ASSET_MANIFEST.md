# ASSET_MANIFEST - Club & Grub

Reference for every file under `assets/`. Generated by `docs/art/pipeline/build_manifest.py` from the registry that the
asset pipeline writes while it builds the files, so the numbers below are measured, not typed by hand.
Proof screenshots composed only from these files: `docs/art/mock_jungle.png`, `mock_cave.png`, `mock_ice.png`,
`mock_volcano.png`, `mock_feast.png`, `mock_boss_brute.png`, `mock_boss_colossus.png` (each 2560x1440 = base viewport x4).

## 1. Art direction and global conventions

**One artist, one look.** Hero, enemies, bosses, NPCs, all terrain, props, parallax layers, HUD, bitmap fonts and most FX come
from a single purpose-made kit: *Superpowers Asset Packs - Prehistoric Platformer* by Pixel-boy / Sparklin Labs (CC0). It is
chunky cartoon pixel art with a 2 px dark-brown outline (`#272018`), flat cel shading and a warm palette. Everything added from
other packs was chosen or processed to sit inside that look (dark 2 px outline, flat shading): 16 px food / gem icons at
integer 2x, 32 px giant food at 2x with an added `#272018` outline, carved-stone touch buttons at 2x recoloured to sandstone.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Base viewport | **640 x 360 art px** (16:9) = **320 x 180 logical px** x `ART_SCALE` **2** (PHYSICS.md 15.3) |
| Tile size | **32 x 32 art px** = 16 x 16 logical px; 20 x 11.25 tiles visible (the original shows 20 x 11) |
| Hero size | body 44 x 56 art px (22 x 28 logical) = 15.6 % of the screen height, i.e. about 1/6 as in Prehistorik 2 |
| Integer window scales | 1280x720 = x2, 1920x1080 = x3, 2560x1440 = x4, 3840x2160 = x6 |
| Texture filter | Nearest everywhere, no mipmaps, no texture compression artefacts (import PNGs lossless) |
| Sheet layout | uniform grid, frames numbered row-major from 0 (`frame = row * columns + column`), ready for `Sprite2D.hframes / vframes` |
| Actor pivot | **(cell_w / 2, cell_h - 16)** = the feet point of PHYSICS.md 2 (bottom-centre). `centered = false`, `offset = (-cell_w/2, -(cell_h-16))`; `flip_h` mirrors correctly because the pivot is horizontally centred. Bosses at 2x use `cell_h - 32`. |
| Facing | every actor sheet faces **right**; exceptions: `bat` (front view) and `colossus` (faces left, lives in the right wall) |
| Item / object / prop pivot | bottom-centre of the cell unless the table says otherwise |
| Collision boxes | given in logical px around the feet point: `left = x - w/2`, `top = y - h` (PHYSICS.md 2.1 box test). They are suggestions measured from the art. |
| Pixel density | native 1x of the anchor pack. Deliberate integer upscales (nearest neighbour): bosses 2x, 16 px icons 2x, giant food 2x, touch buttons 2x. Nothing is scaled by a non-integer factor. |

Why 640 x 360 and not 320x180 / 384x216 / 480x270: the chosen art is drawn at twice the density of 16 px-tile packs (hero 45x57 px,
32 px terrain modules). At 640x360 it reproduces the original framing exactly (20 tile columns, hero 1/6 of the height); at 480x270 only
15 x 8.4 tiles would fit and the hero would fill 21 % of the height. Game logic still runs in the original's 320x180 logical pixels:
simulate in logical px and multiply positions by `ART_SCALE = 2` when placing sprites, exactly as PHYSICS.md 15.3 describes.

File naming: lower_snake_case; `_b` = second palette of the same sheet layout; audio `_a.._d` = interchangeable variants.

- Slopes: `slope45_*` rises 16 logical px over one tile; `gentle_*` rises 8 px per tile (low + high halves). Put the matching `under_*` tile directly below each slope tile. - `spikes_floor` / `spikes_ceiling` are deadly; `hang_*`, `small_block`, `back_*` have no collision.

### 13.4 Loudness, peaks and the mix

Measured by `tools/audio_loudness.py` (decodes every file to float exactly as the game's decoders do, so values above 0 dBFS are visible; ITU-R BS.1770-4 K-weighting via pyloudnorm; true peak 4x oversampled). `scripts/core/audio_table.gd` holds the resulting `volume_db` per file; run `tools/audio_loudness.py --write` after adding or replacing a file.

- **Music** (and jingles) play at **-18 LUFS integrated**. The masters ranged from -6.9 LUFS (`level_extra_a`) to -20.4 LUFS (`bonus_room_b`), a 13.5 LU spread; 9 of the 21 music files decode above 0 dBFS sample peak (up to +1.3 dBFS, `level_cave_a.ogg`) and 13 above 0 dBTP true peak (up to +2.4 dBTP, `password_screen_a.ogg`).
- **Effects** play at **-14 LUFS over their loudest 200 ms** (short-window loudness: the EBU 400 ms momentary window over-rates the boost very short clicks need), i.e. a few LU above the music so that they cut through it. Per-role offsets: footsteps -10 LU, fire / lava ambience loops -12 LU and the blizzard loop -8 LU (loops measured by integrated loudness), menu cursor -3 LU, tally tick -4 LU, scenery hits -2 LU, boss roar and boss defeated +2 LU.
- **No clipping**: every gain is capped so that the file's true peak stays at or below **-1 dBTP** (this limits `land_a`, `menu_move_a`, `club_hit_a` and `breakable_smash_ice_a`, which therefore play slightly below their target), and the `Master` bus has a hard limiter (ceiling -0.5 dB) for sounds that overlap.
- The user's volume sliders (music 0.8 by default = -1.9 dB) apply on top of these values.

## 16. Known gaps

- No sound was auditioned by ear (the pipeline has no audio output): picks rest on the audio scout's measurements; a human listen-through of `assets/audio` is still required, and `volume_db` values are starting points.
- The Rooted Guardian (optional tree-stump mid-boss, GAMEPLAY 6.2) has no dedicated sprite.
- Bosses are pixel-doubled (4 px outlines next to the 2 px outlines of everything else); no boss-sized art exists in the anchor style.
- The hero has no dedicated skid, out-of-breath or idle-fidget animation: use `land` for the skid and `idle` at double speed for panting.
- Terrain has no drawn inner-corner tiles and one top variant per set; long flat stretches repeat every 32 px (break them up with props and `fill_b`).
- Hang-glider: one static overlay sprite (no inflate / fold animation).
- Feast Land has no prop folder of its own: decorate it with `sprites/items/giant_bonus.png` cells and food sprites.
- World-map level markers and the Beginner-wall castle picture are not drawn; compose them from `ui/world_map_background.png`, `ui/icons.png` and village props.

- **Guard / Shellback height vs. 3-row halls.** At the 1x DESIGN asks for, the RPG reptile stands 109 art px (about 54 logical) from feet to horn tip; its head top is 98 art px (49 logical) up. ### 17.9 Known gaps (2.0, art-A)

- See-saw: only the wood plank (lengths 3-6). The mushroom see-saw of 6-2 and the ice floes of Floe Rink are phase-2 skins (recolours of `seesaw_plank.png`).
- The pulley wheel and rope, the revive egg's oval and the two petroglyph figures of the x2 tablet are drawn by the pipeline in the anchor palette (no pack has them); everything else is recoloured / composited pack art.
- `hero_palettes.json` is reference data (shader contract, colour names, UI colours, pattern list). If `hero_palette.gd` (player-A) reads it at runtime, check that the export carries it (an `include_filter` entry by core-A); otherwise keep the few values it needs as constants. The LUT / atlas PNGs are ordinary textures.
- No emote bubbles, versus corner portraits, crown, sundial or hit sparks per player yet (E.9; phase 2, P2.11).
- Belt icons crop the handle end of the longer weapons (club, spear) at the cell edge; the heads stay whole.
- The egg's hatch frames show the hero curled in the 1.0 spots (the pattern step is off for non-hero sheets).
